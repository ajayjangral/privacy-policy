CapsuleShot Privacy Policy

Developer: Ajay Jangral

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapsuleShot ("the app") handles information when you use it on Windows. In short: CapsuleShot has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own, and it never uploads your screenshots. Your pictures stay on your PC.

1. What the app handles

CapsuleShot reads your screen only when you start a capture (shortcut, widget button or area selection). Each screenshot is saved to a folder on your PC and copied to the Windows clipboard so you can paste it with Ctrl + V. The image stays on the clipboard until another app or action replaces it. A screenshot can contain anything visible on your screen, such as messages, passwords or financial details, so check it before you capture or share it.

With All Displays on, one screenshot covers every connected screen. With the pen tool on, you can draw, blur or hide parts of a screenshot before it is saved. With Multi-page capture on, the app captures the same area each time you press capture and joins the pictures into one image, or a PDF with one page per capture. It never clicks, types or scrolls for you.

2. What is stored on your PC

  • Your screenshots: saved in a folder named CapsuleShot (by default inside your Pictures folder; if you choose another location, CapsuleShot creates its folder inside it).
  • Settings: your preferences (widget style, colours, shortcuts and so on) in the app's local user-data folder.
  • Library previews: small preview copies of your images, so the Library opens fast. A preview is removed when its image is deleted from the save folder.
  • Text search index: see section 3.
  • Pro status: only whether Pro is active and when it ends (see section 5).
  • Error log: a small log with no screenshots and no screen content, to help fix problems. It leaves your PC only if you choose to copy the diagnostics text and email it to support yourself.

3. Text search inside images (OCR)

So you can search inside your images, CapsuleShot reads the text in the images of your save folder on your PC and keeps it in a local index file. Nothing is uploaded. Because screenshots can show private information, the index can contain names, numbers or messages that were visible in your images. You can turn this off at any time in Settings (Search text inside images): the app then stops reading your images and deletes the index file straight away. The English language data is included with the app; if it cannot be found, the OCR component may download that one language file once (a download only: none of your images or text is sent).

4. Sharing

CapsuleShot does not send your screenshots, text, settings or usage to the developer or to anyone else, and has no cloud upload or account sync. You decide whether to share a saved picture, for example by pasting it into another app. Windows and the apps you paste into have their own privacy rules; for example, if you turn on Windows clipboard history or cloud clipboard, Windows may keep copied images according to that setting.

5. Pro upgrade and payments

CapsuleShot is free to use. An optional Pro upgrade (monthly subscription or lifetime) removes the watermark and unlocks all widget styles. Purchases are made through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft takes the payment, handles billing, taxes and cancellation, and treats your information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive your card or payment details, name or email address.

To know whether Pro is active, the app asks the Microsoft Store on your PC about your licence. This is the only time the app contacts Microsoft, and it sends no screenshots or personal content. The app keeps the answer locally (active or not, and the end date of a subscription) so Pro keeps working for a while without internet. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Microsoft account.

6. Permissions

CapsuleShot only captures still screenshots. It does not record video or audio and does not use your camera, microphone or location. It runs as a normal desktop app with the permissions needed to capture the screen and to save files in the folder you choose. The Microsoft Store version does not add itself to Windows startup.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your screenshots stay in the save folder until you move or delete them; you can delete them from the app's Library or in File Explorer. Settings, previews and the text index stay on your PC until you delete them or remove the app's local user data. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete screenshots you saved outside its own folders, because they are your files.

8. Security

All of this data stays on your device, so keep your Windows account protected and be careful when sharing screenshots. No method of storing data can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

9. Children

CapsuleShot is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's features change, this policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version, and the same text is available in the app under Settings, Info, Privacy policy.
