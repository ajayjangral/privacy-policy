CapsuleShot — Privacy Policy

Developer: Ajay Jangral

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapsuleShot handles information when you use the desktop application. CapsuleShot has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers: it does not send your screenshots or any other data anywhere.

1. Information the app handles

CapsuleShot processes screen images only when you start a capture. Each screenshot, including a selected-area screenshot, is also copied to your operating system's clipboard to support pasting with Ctrl + V in compatible apps. The image remains on the clipboard until another app or action replaces it or the clipboard is cleared. Captures may contain anything visible on your screen, so check for private messages, passwords, financial details, or other sensitive content before capturing or sharing them.

With All Displays selected, one screenshot covers every connected screen. With the pen tool on, you can draw on a screenshot before it is saved; nothing is sent anywhere. With Multi-page capture on, the app captures the same area each time you press capture and joins those pictures into one image (or into a PDF, one page per capture) on your PC when you press Save. It does not click, type or scroll for you.

2. Where files and settings are stored

By default, captured files are saved locally in the CapsuleShot folder under your Pictures directory. If you choose another location, CapsuleShot saves your files in a folder named CapsuleShot inside it. App preferences are stored locally in the application's user-data settings file. Recent-capture information is read from the configured save folder. To keep the Library fast, CapsuleShot also keeps small preview copies of your images in the app's local user-data folder; a preview is removed when its image is deleted from the save folder, and all of them are removed with the app's local user data. A small error log (no screenshots, no screen content) is kept in the app's local user-data folder to help fix problems. It leaves your device only if you choose to copy the diagnostics text and email it to support yourself.

Text search in the Library. So you can search inside your images, CapsuleShot reads the text of the images in your save folder on your PC (OCR) and keeps that text in a local index file in the app's user-data folder. Nothing is uploaded. The English language data is included with the app; if it cannot be found, the OCR component may download that one language file once from its public source (a download only: none of your images or text is sent). The index follows the images in your save folder. You can turn this off at any time in Settings (Search text inside images): CapsuleShot then stops reading your images and deletes the text index file straight away. Removing the app's local user data also deletes it completely. Because screenshots can show private information, the index can contain text such as names, numbers or messages that were visible in your images; it stays on your PC.

Image Text Extractor. If you turn on Image Text Extractor in Settings (it is off by default), you can select an area of your screen and CapsuleShot reads the text in that area on your PC (OCR) and copies it to your operating system's clipboard. The selected area is not saved as a screenshot or as a file, and the recognized text is not stored by CapsuleShot or added to the Library text index; it stays on the clipboard until another app or action replaces it or the clipboard is cleared. Nothing is uploaded. It uses the same bundled English OCR data as text search. The selected area can show private information, so check what you paste.

Start with Windows. The Microsoft Store version of CapsuleShot does not add itself to Windows startup. In the version you install directly, this is off by default: the app asks once on first launch and only adds itself to startup if you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

3. Collection and sharing

The app is designed to perform its capture and settings functions locally. This policy does not claim that your operating system, third-party software, or any future integrations never process data. CapsuleShot does not provide a cloud upload or account-sync feature in this build. You decide whether to share saved captures using other apps.

4. Permissions and screen content

CapsuleShot captures screenshots only. It does not record video or audio and does not use your microphone. Check that screenshots do not contain private or sensitive content before sharing them.

5. Retention and deletion

Files remain in the save folder until you move or delete them. Settings remain on your device until you remove the app's local user data. Uninstalling the app may not automatically delete files saved outside its installation folder.

6. Security

Keep your device account protected and be careful when sharing capture files. No local storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children and changes

This app is a general-purpose desktop utility and is not specifically designed to collect children's information. This policy may be updated if app features change; check this section for the latest version and date.
